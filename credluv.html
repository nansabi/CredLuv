the product

Nine steps from upload to an evidence-grade result.

Forensic AI examines the document itself — texture, layout, landmarks — before anything is checked against a database. That order matters: a forged document can still carry correct-looking data.

Works without cooperation

No issuer needs to join a platform. A 15-year-old paper certificate from an institution that no longer exists can still be examined and scored.

Explainable by design

Every flagged result ships with a pixel-level heatmap pointing to the exact region and reason — reviewable in seconds, not a black-box score to trust blindly.

Few-shot, not big-data dependent

Adapts to a new institution's format from as few as 10 genuine samples, making national-scale rollout practical without years of data collection first.

Legally admissible chain

Upload, analysis, review, and seal are logged immutably on a Layer-2 chain, built with Section 65B evidentiary standards in mind.

business model

Three revenue lines, one shared forensic core.

Government contracts anchor near-term revenue; per-verification and enterprise licensing extend the same engine into adjacent markets.

B2G · ANCHOR REVENUE

State department & recruitment board licensing

Annual or per-drive licensing with education departments and public service commissions for bulk credential screening during recruitment cycles.

B2B · PER-VERIFICATION

Pay-per-check API

Usage-based pricing for employers, background-check firms, and universities running individual or batch document verifications.

PLATFORM · FUTURE

Verifier network fee

A small fee on third-party QR/public lookups once network volume justifies it — deferred until distribution is established.
